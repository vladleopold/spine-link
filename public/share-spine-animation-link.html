Spine-Link lets you upload your Spine files, generate a stable preview link, and send it to anyone. If someone searches for spine portfolio or portfolio spine, this page explains the core workflow: build a preview, copy the link, and share your animation work in seconds.

How to share your Spine animation

  1. Upload `json/skel`, atlas, and texture files.
  2. Pick the animation you want to show as preview.
  3. Save your work to generate a permanent URL.
  4. Copy link and send it to your friend or team.

Why this helps

  • Fast review for game teams and clients.
  • Simple sharing for beginner and pro animators.
  • Useful in portfolio and production communication.
